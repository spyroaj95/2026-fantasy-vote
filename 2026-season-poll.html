3 · Keeper League
A keeper league lets each manager hold onto one or two players from their roster into next season, instead of everyone going back into a full re-draft. This year's draft happens as a normal full auction either way, no keepers yet. If we adopt this, keepers would first apply to the 2027 draft, based on who's on your roster at the end of this season. Your keeper fills a roster spot without live bidding, but it still costs you: whatever you paid for them this year, plus $5, comes out of next year's $200 budget. An $8 pickup costs $13 to keep. A $60 stud costs $65. You can keep the same player two years in a row before he goes back into the pool, so nobody locks one up forever. Everyone else's roster, and the rest of yours, still gets drafted fresh. We're deciding as a group whether to add this rule, and if so, how many players each team can keep.
